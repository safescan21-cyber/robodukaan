## Q1. India market size and growth by product type, and leading brands

### Takeaway
Research-firm estimates of India's "industrial automation" market range about 2x (USD 7.6 bn to USD 17-25 bn for 2024-25) because scope definitions differ. Most forecast CAGRs fall between 8% and 10%, with outliers near 14-15%. The component markets a small store sells are much smaller: VFDs about USD 0.5-1.8 bn, PLCs about USD 0.5-1.0 bn, HMIs about USD 0.18 bn, and industrial/automation sensors about USD 0.75-1.3 bn. Each grows at roughly 5-11% a year. I found no reliable India-specific SMPS/industrial power-supply figure. No source gave India brand market shares. Only unranked "key player" lists were available.

## Q2. India industrial MRO market, drive/electronics repair services, and online share

### Takeaway
IMARC sizes India's MRO market at USD 56.1 bn in 2024, growing only about 1.7% a year to USD 66.4 bn by 2033. I found no specific market size for industrial electronics or drive repair services, and no credible current figure for B2B e-commerce penetration of MRO. The only share datapoint is dated: in 2018, Moglix's CEO estimated a 0.5% share for the company in its niche.

## Q3. Policy and market factors 2024-2026

### Takeaway
The main policy levers are: the INR 22,919 crore Electronics Component Manufacturing Scheme (ECMS, approved March 2025); BIS CRS mandatory registration for standalone SMPS of 48 V max output and IT power adaptors under IS 13252 (Part 1); the GST 2.0 rationalisation from 22 Sept 2025, which leaves most HSN 8504 goods at 18%; the Udyam MSME base of over 7.3 crore registrations; and the 2017 IE2 motor QCO, with IE3 status unverified.

### Cited Findings

**PLI / ECMS**
- The Union Cabinet approved the Electronics Component Manufacturing Scheme with a INR 22,919 crore outlay in March 2025. It runs six years with a one-year gestation period, and was notified in April 2025. — [IANS, 28 Mar 2025](https://ianslive.in/cabinet-approves-rs-22919-crore-electronics-component-manufacturing-scheme--20250328163303); [News On AIR](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/centre-notifies-₹22919-cr-electronics-component-scheme-to-boost-atmanirbhar-bharat) [GOVT broadcaster]
- First-tranche approvals were 24 applications with INR 12,704 crore of investment. A third tranche approved 22 proposals with a projected INR 41,863 crore of investment. — [FoneArena ECMS tag](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/tag/electronics-component-manufacturing-scheme)
- Approved component projects worth about USD 4.6 bn (Samsung, Tata Electronics, Foxconn and others) are expected to create about 34,000 direct jobs — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/economy-and-policy/centre-approves-electronic-component-projects-worth-46-billion)

**BIS Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS)**
- BIS Scheme II (Registration Scheme) covers electronics and IT goods notified by MeitY, including IS 13252 (Part 1):2010 — [BIS Scheme II page](https://www.bis.gov.in/product-certification/products-under-compulsory-certification/scheme-ii-registration-scheme) [GOVT]
- Standalone SMPS with output voltage of 48 V max, under IS 13252 (Part 1):2010, has been under CRS since 1 April 2021 (item 59 per consultant lists) — [Agile Regulatory](https://www.agileregulatory.com/blogs/bis-crs-registration-for-standalone-switch-mode-power-supplies-smps-with-output-voltage-48v-max-is-13252-part-1-2010); [Aleph India](https://alephindia.in/standalone-switch-mode-power-supplies.php)
- Power adaptors for IT equipment fall under IS 13252 (Part 1); A/V power adaptors fall under IS 616; household appliance adaptors fall under IS 302 (Part 1) — [EVTL India](https://evtlindia.com/bis-crs/bis-certification-for-power-adaptors-for-it-equipment-is-13252-part-1-2010); [IEEE presentation, 2019](https://ewh.ieee.org/r6/ocs/pses/India%20BIS%20&%20WPC_SPCE-2019_Final.pdf)
- Consultant lists state CRS covers about 76 products; counts vary by revision — [Etherbit](https://www.etherbit.in/company/compliances/bis-compulsory-registration-scheme)

**GST / HSN**
- Most goods under HSN 8504 (transformers, static converters, inverters, power supplies) are at the standard 18% GST after the 56th GST Council meeting (Notification 9/2025-CT(Rate), effective 22 Sept 2025) — [Tally Solutions](https://tallysolutions.com/gst/hsn-code-8504-product-classification-gst-rate-business-filing-guide/)
- Some 8504 sub-entries carry a 5% or 18% split, for example solar inverters at 5% when supplied for solar systems — [Patron Accounting 85044010](https://www.patronaccounting.com/hsn-code/85044010); [QuickEstimate](https://quickestimate.co/blog/gst-rate-solar-inverter)
- HSN 8537 covers boards and panels for electric control, which is where PLCs are typically classified; no rate was confirmed in the sources — [Mark IT Solutions Ch.85](https://markitsolutions-website.pages.dev/hsn/chapter-85)

**MSME / Udyam**
- Over 7.3 crore enterprises registered on Udyam and the Udyam Assist Platform between 1 Jul 2020 and 17 Dec 2025: about 4.37 crore on Udyam and 2.92 crore on UAP — [MSME Ministry Year-End Review 2025, via The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/business/msme-sector-achieves-key-milestones-in-2025-udyam-registrations-cross-7-3-crore-msme-ministry-1503532923.html); [Aviation-Defence Universe reprint](https://www.aviation-defence-universe.com/year-end-review-2025-ministry-of-micro-small-medium-enterprises/)
- 7.28 crore per a minister's reply in Parliament (Dec 2025) — [KNN India](https://knnindia.co.in/news/newsdetails/msme/over-728-cr-msmes-registered-on-udyam-portal-since-2020-mos). The total was 6.63 crore as of 31 Jul 2025 — [a2ztaxcorp](https://a2ztaxcorp.net/msme-growth-boost-6-63-crore-msmes-registered-on-udyam-udyam-assist-govt-initiatives-explained/). That implies about 0.67 crore net additions in about 4.5 months [ESTIMATE, my arithmetic].

**Motor efficiency norms**
- The 2017 QCO made IE2 the minimum efficiency class for new LV motors, effective 1 Oct 2017, and also blocks substandard imports — [ABB article](https://new.abb.com/motors-generators/iec-low-voltage-motors/articles/india-bans-low-efficiency-motors-to-save-energy-and-cut-emissions); [ICA India whitepaper](https://copperindia.org/oguploads/knowledge_center/High_Efficiency_Motors_Accelerated_Replacement_Programme_WHITEPAPER0.pdf)
- BEE labelling is "encouraging" a move toward IE3/IE4; this is industry commentary, not a cited mandate — [IMARC energy-efficient motors](https://www.imarcgroup.com/india-energy-efficient-industrial-motors-market); [Powerline, Apr 2025](https://powerline.net.in/2025/04/08/gaining-traction-increasing-demand-for-energy-efficient-motors-and-drives/)

### Inferences
- For a store selling SMPS/adapters: DIN-rail industrial SMPS above 48 V output, and units built into equipment, may fall outside CRS item 59. Standalone SMPS of 48 V or less, and IT adaptors, need a BIS R-number. Sellers should verify each SKU against the current MeitY list.
- ECMS targets passive and active components and sub-assemblies (largely for consumer, IT and telecom). It shapes the domestic supply of parts inside drives and power supplies, rather than finished automation products directly.

### Gaps
- Import duties and restrictions on Chinese electronics: I did not retrieve citable current Basic Customs Duty (BCD) rates for HSN 8504.40 / 8537 / 9032, or details of 2024-26 anti-dumping or other China-specific measures affecting VFDs, PLCs or SMPS. Press Note 3 (2020) on FDI from land-border countries is relevant background, but I did not cite it in this session.
- I could not confirm the exact GST rate for HSN 8537 (PLCs/panels) and 9032 (automatic regulating instruments) after GST 2.0. They are believed to be 18%, but this is unverified here.
- I found no citable 2024-26 data on manufacturing capex or PMI trends, and no DPIIT Make in India statistics.
- I could not confirm whether an IE3 mandate (QCO/BEE MEPS) is in force for LV motors as of 2026.

## Q4. Key growth drivers and headwinds

### Takeaway
The drivers cited across sources are: energy efficiency (IE2 QCO, BEE labelling, drives paired with motors); Industry 4.0 / smart manufacturing; growth in pharma, food & beverage and automotive; the ECMS/PLI component push; and a fast-formalising MSME base. Headwinds are fragmented, price-sensitive demand, wide uncertainty in market sizing, and slow growth in overall MRO spend.

### Cited Findings
- Industrial operators increasingly pair high-efficiency motors with VFDs; the VFD advantage is operational efficiency — [Powerline, Apr 2025](https://powerline.net.in/2025/04/08/gaining-traction-increasing-demand-for-energy-efficient-motors-and-drives/)
- PLC demand is driven by manufacturing, automotive, F&B and pharma — [Mobility Foresights](https://mobilityforesights.com/?p=121464)
- Siemens India is adding drives for solar pumps, electrolysers and data-centre cooling, which are new end-markets — [Expert Market Research](https://www.expertmarketresearch.com/reports/india-ac-drives-market)
- Smart-sensor growth (11.2% CAGR) is outpacing conventional automation sensors (8.8%) — [IMARC smart sensors](https://www.imarcgroup.com/india-industrial-smart-sensors-market); [IMARC automation sensors](https://www.imarcgroup.com/india-industrial-automation-sensors-market)
- ABB India hit record orders in CY2025 (+8%), with drive products cited as a strength — [ABB Q4 2025 release](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/ABB_19022026174454_Press_ReleaseQ42025.pdf)
- Not all in-service induction motors can be combined with a VFD, which limits retrofit demand — [Powerline, 2020](https://powerline.net.in/2020/07/10/driving-efficiency/) (older)

### Inferences
- A China+1 shift and ECMS-driven electronics plant capex should raise automation demand in the electronics and EMS clusters (Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, UP, Gujarat). I did not find a quantified source in this session [ESTIMATE].
- Of the product types reviewed, the 8-11% CAGRs for drives, HMIs and sensors mean these segments grow faster than broad MRO (1.7%, IMARC). That favours a store weighted toward automation components over generic MRO.

### Gaps
- I found no quantified China+1 impact on Indian automation demand.
- I found no data on the grey market or on price pressure from Chinese brands (INVT, Inovance, Delta's China-made lines) in India.
