# Online Store Competitors for an Indian B2B Industrial Electronics Store

Research date: 7 November 2026. Method: web search snippets (WebSearch). Direct page fetches were blocked by the session network policy (dnatechindia.com and multibagg.ai failed to resolve), so **prices are as shown in search-indexed listings, retrieved 7 Nov 2026. The pages may have been indexed earlier, so check them live before quoting.** Marketplace listings (IndiaMART, TradeIndia, Aajjo) are dealer quotes, are often ex-GST, and are often stale.

## 1. Horizontal B2B MRO platforms: Moglix, Industrybuying (IB MonotaRO), OfBusiness, Bizongo

### Takeaway
Moglix (about Rs 4,964 cr FY24 revenue) and OfBusiness (about Rs 19,296 cr FY24) are large, but their revenue comes mostly from enterprise and raw-material procurement, not from a deep catalogue of electronics spares. Industrybuying is the most retail-like B2B shop. It offers COD on some items and "IB Credit", 30-day pay-later credit for buyers with a GST number. Moglix has a weak public reputation: Trustpilot about 2/5, and recurring complaints about fakes and cancellations.

### Cited Findings

- Moglix FY24 loss was about Rs 189 cr. I found no FY25 figures. — [Indian Startup Times](https://www.indianstartuptimes.com/?p=25942); [DealStreetAsia](https://media.dealstreetasia.com/stories/moglix-fy-2024-earnings-415860)

- **IB Credit:** This is pay-later credit for eligible business customers. Buyers need an approved business account with a GST number and pay within 30 days of delivery. - **OfBusiness:** FY24 revenue was Rs 19,296 cr, up from Rs 15,343 cr. It has converted to a public company ahead of an IPO. - **Bizongo:** FY23 revenue was Rs 166.86 cr per Entrackr, while Inc42 says Rs 185 cr. The sources conflict. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/company/bizongo/financials/)

### Inferences
- OfBusiness and Bizongo sell raw materials, packaging, and credit-led procurement to enterprises. They are not direct competitors for spares such as SMPS, drive boards, or PLC modules. Moglix and Industrybuying are the relevant horizontal competitors.
- Industrybuying's mix of COD and 30-day credit for GST buyers sets the bar for small workshops and OEMs. A new player probably needs at least COD plus GST invoices, and credit for repeat accounts.
- Moglix's complaints about cancellations and fakes create room for a specialist that promises genuine, in-stock parts with a clear source.

### Gaps
- FY25 revenue for Moglix, Industrybuying (IB MonotaRO MCA filings), OfBusiness, and Bizongo was not found. Neither were Moglix's published credit or COD terms.
- I did not verify how deep Moglix's and Industrybuying's catalogues go in automation (VFD, PLC, HMI SKU counts), because page fetches were blocked.

## 2. Electronics and component e-stores: who targets industrial vs hobby buyers

### Takeaway
Robu.in (Macfos Ltd, listed) is the largest Indian-owned online component store, with about Rs 312 cr FY26 revenue. It is hobby- and maker-led but growing into drones and B2B, and it sells generic or Fotek-type industrial parts (SSRs, load cells) cheaply. DNA Technology and Probots stock some industrial items such as Mean Well DIN SMPS and S-type load cells, with GST shown. element14 and RS serve engineers and procurement buyers with multi-day delivery from Asian hubs. None of them focuses on the industrial maintenance and repair buyer.

### Cited Findings
- **Macfos (Robu.in):**
  - FY26 audited consolidated revenue was about Rs 311.74 cr, PAT Rs 25.65 cr, and EBITDA Rs 38.97 cr. — [Whalesbook](https://www.whalesbook.com/corporate-news/English/tech/Macfos-Reports-FY26-indian-rupee311-Cr-Revenue-indian-rupee25-Cr-Profit-After-Correction/69f2050f08235f0c2a17b911)
  - The FY25 consolidated base was about Rs 255 cr, which included a one-off bulk sale of about Rs 71 cr in H1 FY25. Management cited about 67% like-for-like growth. — [Multibagg](https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/macfos-q4-fy26-results-cmonpqu41pccgp50jrkquwegr); [ScanX](https://scanx.trade/stock-market-news/companies/macfos-limited-resubmits-consolidated-audited-financial-results-for-fy26-after-correcting-clerical-error-in-cash-flow-statement/40137458)
  - Q1 FY27 revenue from operations was about Rs 81.34 cr. — [Multibagg](https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/macfos-q1-97880)
  - It is listed on BSE (543787). — [AlphaSpread](https://www.alphaspread.com/security/bse/543787/investor-relations)
  - Coverage describes a "two-engine strategy" (e-commerce plus drones). — [Multibagg](https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/macfos-fy26-93121); [Whalesbook](https://www.whalesbook.com/corporate-news/English/tech/Macfos-Eyes-indian-rupee311-Cr-FY26-Revenue-Boosted-by-E-commerce-and-Drones/69f1a21208235f0c2a15ee79)
- **Robu.in reviews (Trustpilot, mixed):**
  - Complaints: short-shipped items with the ticket closed, express-delivery fee not refunded after a late delivery, a damaged order with only a 2-day warranty, and an item that differed from the listing.
  - Praise: parts matched descriptions, good packaging, and responsive support.
  - [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/robu.in?page=1); [Trustpilot p3](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/robu.in?page=3)
- **DNA Technology (dnatechindia.com):** Lists Mean Well EDR-120-24 at Rs 1,958 + GST (about Rs 2,310 incl. GST) with 2–8 day delivery across India. — [DNA Technology](https://www.dnatechindia.com/meanwell-edr-120-24-smps-buy-in-india.html)
- **Probots:**
  - Lists an "industrial grade" 500 kg S-type load cell at Rs 4,899 incl. GST (Rs 4,151.69 + Rs 747.30 GST). It was out of stock when crawled.
  - The page invites contact for higher quantities, so bulk pricing is by RFQ.
  - [Probots](https://probots.co.in/industrial-grade-load-cell-s-type-500-kg-weight-sensor-s-bar.html)
- **element14 India:** A directory lists "0+4 day" delivery across India, and an older article describes 0+3 day delivery to major cities from its Asia warehouse. Both are likely outdated. — [Industrial Automation India](https://www.industrialautomationindia.in/shwprvitm/205/element14-India-Pvt-Ltd.-/show-preview); [ElectronicsB2B](https://www.electronicsb2b.com/?p=19941)
- **RS India:** Offered free doorstep delivery within 4–5 business days, per a 2022-era interview. — [BISinfotech](https://www.bisinfotech.com/?p=143406)
- **Electronicscomp:** Only one negative Trustpilot mention was found, with no detail. — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/robu.in?page=1)

### Inferences
- **Hobby and maker-led:** Robu, Robocraze, Probots, ElectronicsComp and Evelta compete on low price and breadth of generic parts. Their warranties are short (Robu's 2-day warranty complaint), and they offer little application support.
- **Engineer and procurement-led:** element14, RS, Mouser and DigiKey offer genuine branded parts and datasheets, but deliver in multiple days from overseas hubs. They also don't stock Indian-market drive and PLC spares such as Delta and INVT.
- DNA Technology is the closest to a "small industrial" positioning among the e-stores: it sells Mean Well DIN-rail SMPS and shows GST pricing.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable revenue or funding data for DNA Technology, Tanotis, ElectronicsComp, Probots, Evelta, or element14 India / RS India's India-specific figures.
- I found nothing on India-specific terms for Mouser or DigiKey India (free-shipping thresholds, duties, INR pricing).
- COD, credit and returns policies for these e-stores were not verified because fetches were blocked.

## 3. Industrial automation e-shops and authorised distributor portals

### Takeaway
Branded automation (Delta, Omron, Mean Well) is sold online in India mainly through IndiaMART, TradeIndia and Aajjo dealer listings, which use "get latest price" RFQs and wide price ranges. Some specialist web shops exist: The Engineer Store, Lohiya Automation, and L&T-SuFin, which shows "price on request" for Delta VFDs. Few offer transparent cart-checkout pricing for PLC, VFD and HMI items.

### Cited Findings
- **L&T-SuFin (L&T's B2B portal):** Lists Delta VFD-C2000/EL 1.5 kW drives but shows "Price on request". — [L&T-SuFin](https://lntsufin.com/product/delta-vfd015cb43a-20-415-v-1-5-kw-three-phase-variable-frequency-drive/15840-145)
- **DVP14SS2 sellers:** Listings are mostly on IndiaMART and TradeIndia with "Get Latest Price" or "Call Now". I found no Indian retail add-to-cart listing. — [IndiaMART](https://m.indiamart.com/proddetail/logic-controller-12495373897.html)
- **The Engineer Store (theengineerstore.in):** Sells Delta DOP-107 HMIs as "original, authentic" with a 7-day manufacturing-defect warranty and 7–8 day delivery across India. — [The Engineer Store](https://www.theengineerstore.in/te/products/dop-107eg-7-800-600)
- **Lohiya Automation:** Lists Mean Well DR-120-24 and Delta DVP14SS11R2 online. — [Lohiya Automation](https://www.lohiyaautomation.com/mean-well-dr-120-24-power-supply-8412961.html); [Lohiya Automation](https://www.lohiyaautomation.com/delta-electronics-dvp14ss11r2-programmable-logic-controller-9630572.html)
- **Synchronics Electronics (Vadodara):**
  - Advertises pan-India door-step pickup for VFD and servo board-level repair, a no-fix-no-fee policy, and free diagnosis.
  - Turnaround is 48–72 h for standard faults and 5–8 working days for involved repairs.
  - Board repair costs about Rs 12,000–22,000 on one page. Warranty is 6 or 12 months; the pages conflict.
  - No exchange-board programme was found.
  - These are vendor marketing claims. — [Synchronics](https://synchronics.co.in/manufacturers/drive-technology); [Synchronics](https://synchronics.co.in/items/vfd-fr-a740-00770-in)

### Inferences
- The automation segment is fragmented and RFQ-driven. A web shop with live INR prices incl. GST, stock status and fast dispatch for common Delta, INVT, Omron and Mean Well SKUs would differentiate itself.
- Repair is offered by separate service firms rather than by the e-stores, so combining spares, repair and exchange looks like an open niche.

### Gaps
- I did not research official Schneider, ABB or Siemens distributor e-portals or OEM web shops (for example, whether Delta India has an online store).
- I found no data on authorised vs grey-market sourcing among marketplace sellers.

## 5. Customer review themes and unmet needs (gaps)

### Takeaway
Across platforms the complaints are fakes or wrong items, cancellations after a low price is shown, slow refunds, short-shipped orders, and short warranties. None of the online stores reviewed combines spares sales with board repair, exchange units, fault-code help, or local-language technical support. Repair is offered by separate firms such as Synchronics, and I found no exchange-board programme anywhere.

### Cited Findings
- **Moglix:** Fakes or cheap substitutes, non-delivery, dispatch delays, and "out of stock" cancellations after a low price was shown. — [Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/www.moglix.com?page=8); [ConsumerComplaints.in](https://www.consumercomplaints.in/moglix-b115771)
- **Robu.in:** Short shipments, a late express delivery without a fee refund, damaged goods with a 2-day warranty, and items not as described. Some reviews praise packaging and support. — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/robu.in?page=3)
- **Specialist repair:** Synchronics offers pickup, board-level VFD repair and no-fix-no-fee, but no exchange units were found. — [Synchronics](https://synchronics.co.in/manufacturers/drive-technology)
- **Warranties:** The Engineer Store gives only a 7-day manufacturing-defect warranty on HMIs. — [The Engineer Store](https://www.theengineerstore.in/te/products/dop-107eg-7-800-600)

### Inferences (gaps a new player could fill)
- **Repair and exchange:** Drive, SMPS and HMI board repair plus exchange or advance-replacement boards, sold alongside new spares in one store.
- **Fault-code help:** Tech support in Hindi and regional languages (WhatsApp or phone), with fault-code lookup pages for Delta, INVT and Mitsubishi VFDs and PLC wiring help. This is not seen at any competitor reviewed.
- **Commercial terms:** Genuine-part guarantees with sourcing proof, live stock, and same or next-day dispatch, plus COD and GST invoices. Add 30-day credit for repeat MSME buyers to match Industrybuying.
- **Warranty:** Longer warranties than the 2–7 days seen at Robu and The Engineer Store.

### Gaps
- MouthShut, Google reviews and Reddit threads were not retrievable in this pass. No Reddit evidence was found, and sentiment on Industrybuying, element14, RS and DNA Technology is unresearched.
- I did not verify whether any competitor offers local-language support or fault-code content. The gap claims above come from absence in search results, not from a page-by-page audit.
