# Lowest-Cost Customer Acquisition Channels for PCBDukaan (Indian B2B industrial electronics repair + parts)

Research date: 2026-11-07. Method: web search snippets (15 searches/fetches). Direct fetches of refrens.com and nicodigital.com failed (host not reachable from this sandbox), and indiamart.com was not fetched, so the IndiaMART and Google Ads CPC figures come from search snippets of those pages. Each item is marked **[Sourced]** (figure appears in a cited page or snippet) or **[Estimate]** (my inference). Where sources disagree, both are given.

## Q1. B2B marketplaces: IndiaMART, TradeIndia, ExportersIndia, JustDial. Free vs paid, prices, BuyLeads, seller experience

### Takeaway
A free IndiaMART catalogue costs nothing but gets no weekly BuyLead allocation. The cheapest paid entry is Mini Dynamic Catalog, at roughly ₹28,000–35,000/yr plus GST, or about ₹3,000/month on monthly billing. It gives 7–10 BuyLeads a week, which works out to about ₹55–90 per lead before GST [Estimate]. TrustSEAL Pro costs ₹45,000–50,000/yr and Maximiser Pro ₹85,000/yr. Seller reviews are sharply split: shared and irrelevant leads come up again and again. TradeIndia, ExportersIndia and JustDial cost about the same (₹24k–75k/yr) and none publishes a reliable price list.

### Cited Findings
**IndiaMART prices (they conflict; confirm with an IndiaMART sales rep):**
- Mini Dynamic Catalog (MDC): ₹35,000 for 1 yr, ₹72,000 for 2 yrs, ₹90,000 for 3 yrs, per a third-party page quoting IndiaMART's help centre as updated 2 June 2026. The same page says IM Star Pro, IM Leader Pro and IM Industry Leader are quote-only, priced by a sales rep. [Sourced] — [swadeshiapps.com/e-commerce/indiamart](https://swadeshiapps.com/e-commerce/indiamart)
- MDC: ₹3,000/month or ₹28,000/yr. TrustSEAL: ₹45,000 for 1 yr, ₹60,000 for 2 yrs, ₹80,000 for 3 yrs. Third-party guide, mid-2026. [Sourced, but conflicts with the line above] — [Refrens: Overview of all IndiaMART packages](https://www.refrens.com/grow/detailed-overview-and-analysis-of-all-indiamart-packages/)
- TrustSEAL Pro: ₹50,000 for 1 yr, ₹80,000 for 2 yrs, ₹1,00,000 for 3 yrs, all excluding taxes (official help page, undated). [Sourced] — [IndiaMART help: TrustSEAL](https://dev-help.indiamart.com/knowledge-base/trustseal/)
- Only paid suppliers can be TrustSEAL verified. Verification is free with Maximiser and higher plans (help page updated 6 Mar 2025). [Sourced] — [IndiaMART help: TrustSEAL](https://helpadmin.indiamart.com/knowledge-base/trustseal/comment-page-2/)
- Maximiser Pro: ₹85,000 for 1 yr, ₹1,30,000 for 2 yrs, TrustSEAL included. [Sourced] — [IndiaMART help: Maximiser Pro](https://help.indiamart.com/knowledge-base/maximiser-pro/)
- Older (about 2021) MDC + TrustSEAL combo: ₹80,000 for 3 yrs plus GST. Outdated. [Sourced] — [corporate.indiamart.com/mdc-ts-combo](https://corporate.indiamart.com/mdc-ts-combo/)

**IndiaMART BuyLeads:**
- Weekly BuyLead allocation by plan: MDC monthly 7/week, MDC annual 10/week, TrustSEAL Pro 14/week, Maximiser Pro 21/week, IM Star Pro 30/week. Every plan adds 1 daily bonus BuyLead. Leads are allocated on Sunday and lapse on Saturday. [Sourced] — [IndiaMART help: BuyLead allocation](https://help.indiamart.com/knowledge-base/buy-lead-allocation)
- A seller flagged as a PNS (phone) or email defaulter, meaning one who doesn't answer enquiries, is cut to 7 BuyLeads/week on any plan. [Sourced] — [IndiaMART help: PNS defaulter](https://help.indiamart.com/knowledge-base/pns-defaulter)

- A third-party blog puts annual plans at roughly ₹25,000 to ₹1,00,000+, adding that names, prices and credits change and are often negotiated. [Sourced] — [quickestimate.co: solar lead from IndiaMART](https://quickestimate.co/blog/solar-lead-from-indiamart)
- Search snippets show existing IndiaMART listings for "VFD repair service", so competitors already sell this category there. [Sourced] — [m.indiamart.com/anubhavtechnosolutions](https://m.indiamart.com/anubhavtechnosolutions)

**Seller experiences:**
- Trustpilot reviewers report irrelevant enquiries and a single lead "shared with 10-11 sellers at once". [Sourced] — [Trustpilot IndiaMART reviews](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/m.indiamart.com?page=1)
- "Useless buy leads … they don't have any requirements". Reviewers also say the sales team "promises a lot before payment". [Sourced] — [SmartCustomer IndiaMART reviews](https://www.smartcustomer.com/reviews/indiamart.com/not_recommended)
- Some sellers report buyers on a "daily basis" and call the matching useful. [Sourced] — [SmartCustomer](https://www.smartcustomer.com/reviews/indiamart.com?page=3)
- A third-party solar-industry guide says lead quality varies and not every enquiry deserves the same follow-up. [Sourced] — [quickestimate.co](https://quickestimate.co/blog/solar-lead-from-indiamart)

**TradeIndia / ExportersIndia / JustDial:**
- TradeIndia paid packages "reportedly start around ₹30,000/year" per one comparison site and "from INR 74,999" per another. The two conflict. [Sourced] — [moonproduct IndiaMART alternatives 2026](https://www.moonproduct.tech/insights/indiamart-alternatives-2026/); [starleads IndiaMART alternatives](https://starleads.in/guides/indiamart-alternatives/)
- ExportersIndia seller plan from ₹24,000/yr + GST with 6 buy leads/month (comparison site). The official advertise page lists Platinum, Platinum+, Star and Global tiers, but the prices were garbled in extraction. [Sourced] — [moonproduct](https://www.moonproduct.tech/insights/indiamart-alternatives-2026/); [ExportersIndia advertise](https://www.exportersindia.com/advertise/)
- JustDial has no public rate card. A sales executive quotes after you share your number, and the minimum term is 1 yr. [Sourced] — [leadproio JustDial package price 2026](https://leadproio.com/justdial-package-price/)
- An older (about 4-year-old) post cites a JustDial ₹20,000 setup fee plus ₹2,000/month. Likely outdated. [Sourced] — [LinkedIn: TradeIndia vs JustDial](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/comparison-between-tradeindia-vs-justdial-rajan-arya)
- TradeIndia also draws Trustpilot complaints. [Sourced] — [Trustpilot TradeIndia](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/tradeindia.com)

### Inferences
- [Estimate] MDC annual at about ₹28–35k/yr with 10 leads/week (about 520/yr, more with daily bonuses) works out to about ₹55–70 per BuyLead. MDC monthly at ₹3,000 with 7/week (about 30/month) is about ₹100/lead. Leads are shared with many sellers, so the cost per *won* customer is far higher.
- [Estimate] Repair is a high-intent, urgent purchase: a factory with a dead VFD wants it fixed today. The free IndiaMART catalogue is therefore still worth having for inbound "VFD repair [city]" searches. A sensible test is MDC **monthly** (₹3k) for 2–3 months before committing to a year, with response time kept under 15 minutes to avoid PNS-defaulter throttling.
- [Estimate] Skip TrustSEAL, Star and Leader until MDC shows measurable ROI.

### Gaps
- No authoritative 2025–26 IndiaMART price page was fetchable, and the MDC base price conflicts (₹28k vs ₹35k).
- No rupee price per standalone BuyLead top-up was found.
- No JustDial current price was found, and no data on JustDial's effectiveness for industrial B2B.
- No Reddit seller threads surfaced.

## Q2. Google Ads CPC for industrial repair keywords; Google Business Profile

### Takeaway
No published keyword-level Indian CPC exists for "VFD repair", "PLC repair", "SMPS repair" or "servo drive repair". The best proxy is an Indian agency figure of ₹20–70 per click for B2B manufacturing, with a suggested budget of ₹10,000–25,000/month. Global industrial CPL benchmarks ($85–107) are much higher than India and not directly applicable.

### Cited Findings
- India B2B manufacturing CPC is typically ₹20–70, and an agency suggests ₹10,000–25,000/month for B2B manufacturers' search campaigns. The source is a Coimbatore agency page, undated. [Sourced] — [webwonderworks: Google Ads for Coimbatore businesses](https://webwonderworks.pages.dev/blog/google-ads-coimbatore-tips)
- India-wide average CPC across industries runs ₹8–25 in one agency blog and ₹20–150 in another. High-intent verticals such as finance, legal and B2B SaaS cost ₹100–600+. Search results did not clearly say which figure came from which page. [Sourced, attribution uncertain] — [megadigital.ai: Google Ads cost in India](https://megadigital.ai/en/blog/google-ads-cost-in-india/); [upgrowth: Google Ads pricing India 2026](https://upgrowth.in/google-ads-pricing-india-2026/); [owlclaw PPC benchmarks India](https://owlclaw.com/benchmarks/ppc-benchmarks-india)
- B2B IT services in Bangalore cost ₹25–55/click. [Sourced] — [onecity.co.in: Google Ads cost in Bangalore](https://onecity.co.in/blog/google-ads-cost-in-bangalore/)
- India-specific CPC depends on keyword intent, Quality Score, city competition and seasonality, and Quality Score is the biggest lever. The page does not break out industrial repair terms. [Sourced] — [nicodigital: Google Ads cost in India 2026](https://www.nicodigital.com/performance-marketing/google-ads-cost-in-india-cpc-benchmarks/)
- Global manufacturing figures conflict. One cites $1.43 average CPC (Agency Analytics 2025). A 2026 benchmark gives industrial at $4.47 CPC and $107.47 per conversion. A LOCALiQ 2025 figure is $5.70 CPC and $85.63 CPL. [Sourced, conflicting] — [webtonic: Manufacturing Google Ads statistics](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/manufacturing-google-ads-statistics); [visionary-marketing: Google Ads benchmarks 2026](https://visionary-marketing.co.uk/blog/google-ads-benchmarks-2026)
- US all-industry figures: $5.42 median CPC and $66.69 per lead (WordStream/LocaliQ 2026). [Sourced] — [admanage.ai](https://admanage.ai/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-advertise-on-google)

### Inferences
- [Estimate] Niche industrial repair keywords probably have low search volume and moderate competition in India, so a CPC of ₹20–60 is plausible. At ₹20–60/click and a 5–10% click-to-enquiry rate, CPL would be about ₹200–1,200. Treat that as a hypothesis to check in Keyword Planner and with a ₹10k test month.
- [Estimate] Exact and phrase match on "[brand] VFD repair", "servo drive repair near me" and city names (Pune, Chakan, Ahmedabad and so on) should keep CPC low.
- [Estimate] Google Business Profile is free. Most "VFD repair near me" searches show a map pack, so a GBP listing with category "Electronics repair shop", photos of repaired drives, and reviews from factory customers is probably the single cheapest high-intent channel. No India industrial GBP case data was found.

### Gaps
- No published Indian Keyword Planner data for the four target keywords. **Action:** run Keyword Planner, which is free with a Google Ads account, for India and target states.
- No sourced evidence on Google Business Profile results for local industrial services in India.

## Q3. YouTube / Instagram repair videos and WhatsApp: B2B evidence and API costs

### Takeaway
WhatsApp is widely described as the default supplier channel for Indian B2B buyers, and the Business app is free. API marketing messages cost about ₹0.86 each plus GST (Jan 2026 rate), utility and authentication about ₹0.115, and service replies inside 24 hours are free. Evidence that YouTube or Instagram repair videos generate industrial leads in India is anecdotal or agency-written. No credible case study was found.

### Cited Findings
- Since 1 Jul 2025, Meta charges per delivered message by category and recipient country. Service replies within the 24-hour window are free. [Sourced] — [MyOperator support](https://support.myoperator.com/portal/en/kb/articles/whatsapp-has-shifted-to-per-message-pricing-effective-july-1-2025-based-on-message-categories-and-your-recipient-s-country-in-india-there-are-three-paid-categories)
- India marketing messages cost about ₹0.8631 each excluding GST. That is up about 10% from about ₹0.78 on 1 Jan 2026. Utility and authentication cost about ₹0.115. [Sourced] — [Monty Mobile: WhatsApp API pricing India](https://montymobile.com/blogs/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-in-india-inr-rates-gst-and-the-2026-currency-migration-deadline)
- Utility costs ₹0.11–0.16 and is free inside an open service window. [Sourced] — [Robylon](https://www.robylon.ai/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india)
- Mid-2025 rates were marketing about ₹0.88 and utility about ₹0.13. [Sourced] — [getspace](https://getspace.co.in/blog/payments/whatsapp-api-pricing-update-july-2025/)
- AiSensy quotes marketing at about ₹1.09 and utility/auth at ₹0.14–0.15, which may include its platform markup. The BSP platform fee comes on top of Meta's charges. [Sourced] — [AiSensy pricing guide](https://m.aisensy.com/blog/whatsapp-business-pricing/)
- Click-to-WhatsApp ads in B2B India cost ₹200–800 per lead. The source is an agency blog and doesn't name its data. [Sourced, low reliability] — [sotrosinfotech: WhatsApp click-to-chat B2B CPL 2026](https://sotrosinfotech.pages.dev/blog/whatsapp-click-to-chat-ads-b2b-lead-generation-2026/)
- "B2B buyers in India … use WhatsApp to communicate with suppliers", and agencies recommend WhatsApp Business with a catalogue (agency opinion). [Sourced] — [prateeksha: B2B lead gen India website + WhatsApp](https://prateeksha.com/blog/b2b-lead-generation-india-website-whatsapp)
- YouTube has about 462M Indian users and is described as dominant for technical demos and vernacular content. [Sourced, agency blog] — [globpulse: B2B lead generation for Indian manufacturers](https://www.globpulse.com/blog/b2b-lead-generation-strategies-indian-manufacturers)
- 3,500+ WhatsApp lead conversations for a fabric manufacturer. This is textiles, not industrial maintenance. [Sourced] — [Fueler case](https://fueler.io/hatimbohra/generated-35k-lead-conversation-on-whatsapp-for-a-fabric-manufacturer)

### Inferences
- [Estimate] For PCBDukaan the free WhatsApp Business app (catalogue, labels, quick replies, broadcast lists of up to 256 contacts who saved your number) is enough at first. API costs are trivial: 1,000 marketing messages a month cost about ₹860 + GST plus the BSP fee.
- [Estimate] Short "before/after repair" YouTube Shorts and Reels showing a burnt IGBT, a replaced capacitor or a drive tested on load cost little beyond staff time. They mainly build trust and help SEO ("Yaskawa V1000 OC fault repair"), not direct volume. Hindi and Marathi/Gujarati voice-over matches how maintenance technicians search.
- [Estimate] WhatsApp groups of maintenance engineers exist informally (plant, alumni and trade groups). Get in through members rather than spamming. No data on how much they produce.

### Gaps
- No verifiable case study of an Indian industrial repair firm getting leads from YouTube, Instagram or WhatsApp groups.
- Meta's official India rate card was not fetched directly. The figures above come from BSP blogs.

## Q4. Industry associations and trade fairs: fees and stall costs

### Takeaway
IEEMA membership costs about ₹10,000 one-time plus about ₹15,000/yr + GST (2020 form). Automation Expo stalls cost about ₹11,000–13,500 per m² + 18% GST, so a minimum 9 m² shell stall is about ₹1.2–1.4 lakh including GST before travel and collateral. Local MIDC/GIDC estate associations are probably far cheaper, but no fees were found.

### Cited Findings
- IEEMA one-time admission fee is ₹10,000, non-refundable. The annual fee depends on turnover. [Sourced] — [IEEMA: Join](https://ieema.org/?p=20883)
- IEEMA institutional form (2020) shows ₹10,000 admission and ₹15,000 annual subscription, both + 18% GST. [Sourced] — [IEEMA membership form PDF](https://ieema.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Application-Form-for-Institutional-Membership.pdf); [IEEMA membership services](https://ieema.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/IEEMA-Membership-Services-1.pdf)
- Automation Expo brochure: raw space ₹11,000/m², shell ₹11,500/m², and a second pair at ₹13,000 and ₹13,500/m², GST extra. The higher pair is probably Mumbai, but the brochure doesn't say. [Sourced] — [Automation Expo brochure PDF](https://www.automationindiaexpo.com/commonimages/brochure/260820262054529009.pdf); [Automation Expo: Plan your expo](https://automationindiaexpo.com/plan-your-expo)
- Automation Expo South: bare space ₹11,000/m² + 18% GST with a 36 m² minimum, constructed ₹11,500/m² + 18% GST with a 9 m² minimum. [Sourced] — [Automation Expo South PDF](https://south.automationindiaexpo.com/_files/ugd/c76346_d586816d7aad4465906322ba487f7ca7.pdf)
- 2019 rates were ₹10,500 bare and ₹11,000 constructed per m². [Sourced] — [The Machine Maker 2019](https://themachinemaker.com/news/book-your-stalls-today-for-automation-expo-2019/)
- Automation Expo 2025 ran 11–14 Aug at the Bombay Exhibition Centre, Goregaon. [Sourced] — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/mumbai/automation-expo-2025-set-to-kick-off-in-mumbai-amid-global-manufacturing-uncertainty); [Hioki India event](https://www.hioki.com/in-en/news/events/detail/id_1268295.html)

### Inferences
- [Estimate] A 9 m² shell stall at ₹11,500–13,500/m² is ₹1.03–1.22 lakh, or about ₹1.22–1.43 lakh with GST, before travel, printing and staff. That is several times a year of IndiaMART MDC. Walking the show as a **visitor** (usually free registration) and collecting cards from OEM, drive-distributor and panel-builder stalls is the low-cost alternative.
- [Estimate] Local industrial-estate associations (MIDC/Chakan/Bhosari/GIDC) usually charge modest fees and give access to member directories and monthly meets. They are likely the cheapest relationship channel, but this is unverified.

### Gaps
- No fees found for CII, FICCI, Laghu Udyog Bharati or MIDC/GIDC estate associations.
- No IMTEX or Engimach stall rates found. No regional fair rates either (e.g. Industrial Expo Pune, Rajkot machine-tool shows).

## Q5. Referral programmes with technicians/electricians; OEM/dealer partnerships

### Takeaway
Big Indian electrical brands run electrician loyalty schemes: Havells E-Plus/Sampark/Club Energy, the Polycab Experts app, and Digisol Cash Karo. These schemes are built on QR-scan points with cash to the bank account, which shows electricians in India respond to small, instant UPI or bank payouts. No published referral rates for repair-service referrals were found.

### Cited Findings
- Havells E-Plus scheme (21–30 Apr 2025): an electrician had to reach at least 60 points to earn a bonus. PAN was mandatory, with TDS at 20% without it. [Sourced] — [Havells loyalty scheme PDF](https://loyaltyuat.havells.com/NotificationUpload/242_flashAttachment.pdf)
- Havells Sampark app lets channel partners earn and redeem points. Club Energy rewards electricians, retailers and distributors. [Sourced] — [mwm.ai Sampark](https://mwm.ai/apps/sampark/1257644761); [almonds.ai distribution loyalty programs](https://almonds.ai/top-10-distribution-loyalty-programs/)
- Polycab Experts App gives electricians instant bank transfers on QR scans, with tiers, insurance and scholarships (single secondary source). [Sourced] — [buyerr.in electrician loyalty](https://buyerr.in/blogs/electrician-loyalty-program-brand/)
- Digisol Cash Karo: scan a QR code for points, and at 500 points the amount goes to the bank account (launched 2019). [Sourced] — [ITVoice](https://www.itvoice.in/digisol-launches-cash-karo-loyalty-program-to-empower-electricians-across-india)

### Inferences
- [Estimate] A simple PCBDukaan referral offer: a fixed ₹300–1,000 UPI payout, or 5% of the first repair invoice, to any electrician or maintenance technician who brings a paid job. It costs nothing unless a job closes. Keep PAN and TDS rules in mind (194H commission) for payouts above thresholds.
- [Estimate] Partnerships with drive and PLC distributors or machine dealers, who often have no in-house repair capability for out-of-warranty boards, can be a revenue-share (10–15%) white-label repair arrangement. This is unverified and there are no Indian benchmarks.

### Gaps
- No data on referral conversion or typical commission rates for industrial repair referrals in India.
- No sourced examples of OEM or dealer repair partnerships in India.

## Q6. GeM (Government e-Marketplace)

### Takeaway
GeM seller registration is free. The turnover-based caution money (₹5,000, ₹10,000 or ₹25,000) was reportedly dropped in 2025–26, but sources conflict. No source confirmed a dedicated repair-services category for industrial electronics.

### Cited Findings
- Basic seller registration has no fee. Caution money slabs are ₹5,000, ₹10,000 or ₹25,000 by turnover. [Sourced] — [Professional Utilities: GeM seller registration](https://professionalutilities.com/gem-seller-registration.php); [GeM caution money PDF](https://assets-bg.gem.gov.in/resources/upload/shared_doc/caution-money-um-1_1603281718.pdf)
- Recent guides report that the Department of Commerce's 2025-26 Annual Report says caution money was removed for all sellers and service providers and can be withdrawn. Not verified against a primary source. [Sourced, conflicting] — [Agile Regulatory: GeM seller 2026 update](https://www.agileregulatory.com/blogs/how-to-register-on-gem-portal-as-seller---2026-update); [Tender18: GeM caution money](https://tender18.com/blog-details/GeM-Caution-Money)

### Inferences
- [Estimate] It's worth registering because it costs little. PSU and government plants (railway workshops, power plants, defence factories) buy spares and AMC repairs. Expect L1 (lowest-price) bidding and slow payment, so it is an opportunistic channel, not a core one.

### Gaps
- No data on repair-service category availability, bid volumes or win rates for drive/PLC repair on GeM.

**Step-by-step low-budget sequence [Estimate]:**
1. Week 1: claim and optimise Google Business Profile (category, service list per brand of drive/PLC/SMPS, photos, WhatsApp link). Ask the last 20 customers for reviews.
2. Week 1: set up WhatsApp Business with a catalogue for repair services and common spares, quick replies with a pickup/courier process, labels for lead stages, and a broadcast list of existing customers. Post one repair case to Status daily.
3. Week 1–2: create a free IndiaMART, TradeIndia and ExportersIndia listing with keyword-rich product titles ("ABB ACS550 VFD repair", "Siemens S7-300 PLC repair") and a promise to reply within 15 minutes.
4. Week 2: launch a referral card for electricians and maintenance technicians (₹500 UPI per closed job) and distribute it via couriers, at the plant gate and on WhatsApp.
5. Week 2–4: run Keyword Planner and spend ₹10k on exact/phrase-match city + brand repair terms. Track calls and WhatsApp clicks.
6. Month 2–3: test IndiaMART MDC monthly (₹3k). Keep it only if cost per closed repair is below the gross margin of one job.
